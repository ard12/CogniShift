MANGALORE REFINERY & PETROCHEMICALS LIMITED (MRPL)
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: K-101 CENTRIFUGAL RECYCLE GAS COMPRESSOR
DOCUMENT ID: SOP-MRPL-K101-REV-04 | CLASSIFICATION: RESTRICTED / PLANT OPERATIONS

1. EQUIPMENT DESCRIPTION & OPERATIONAL ENVELOPE:
Compressor K-101 is a multi-stage centrifugal recycle gas compressor driven by a 4,200 kW, 6.6 kV electric motor (M-101).
Service: Recycle Gas (High Pressure Hydrogen-rich gas).
Normal Suction Pressure: 30.0 to 35.0 bar(g) (Nominal: 32.5 bar).
Normal Discharge Pressure: 64.0 to 70.0 bar(g) (Nominal: 68.2 bar).
Maximum Working Pressure: 78.0 bar(g).
Normal Operating Speed: 10,800 to 11,400 RPM (Design: 11,200 RPM).
Radial Vibration Limit: Normal 1.2-2.8 mm/s RMS, Warning > 3.5 mm/s, Trip > 4.5 mm/s.
Bearing Temperature DE (TT-204): Normal 72.0-88.0 °C, Warning > 90.0 °C, Trip > 95.0 °C.
Lube Oil Supply Pressure (PT-108): 4.6-5.4 bar(g), Trip < 3.2 bar(g).
Dry Gas Seal Differential (PDIT-101): Normal 3.8-4.8 bar delta, Trip < 2.8 bar delta.

2. PRE-STARTUP VERIFICATION & PERMITTING:
- Ensure auxiliary lube oil pump is running and lube oil temperature is between 38°C and 45°C.
- Confirm seal gas differential pressure (PDIT-101) exceeds 3.5 bar delta across all seals.
- Confirm motor-operated suction isolation valve MOV-101 is in remote-auto mode, fully stroked, and confirmed OPEN.
- Verify cooling water flow is verified at nominal 120 m3/h.
- Confirm all 14 safety trip channels report HEALTHY on the ESD-101 Safety Instrumented System.

3. FOUR-EYES AUTHORIZATION & HIGH-RISK ACTIONS:
In compliance with SIH-26117 air-gapped industrial governance, any modification of operating parameters,
service restart, or emergency bypass of compressor K-101 requires strict Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)
Dual Supervisor Authorization (Four-Eyes Principle).
Tool 'restart_service' for K-101 or M-101 is classified as service_interrupting.
Automated execution is strictly prevented; the permit must be signed by two independent credentialed supervisors
(e.g., Vicky S. and Jane D.) before execution.

4. EMERGENCY TRIP & FLARE VENTING:
In the event of uncontrolled vibration (> 4.5 mm/s RMS) or loss of seal gas differential (< 2.8 bar delta):
1. Breaker 52-K101 opens in under 40 milliseconds, cutting 6.6 kV feed to drive motor M-101.
2. Suction isolation valve MOV-101 closes immediately with a stroke time under 6.0 seconds.
3. Fast-acting depressurization valve XV-101 actuates to vent compressor gas to the Flare-Header.
4. Emergency nitrogen sweep valve XV-105 opens to dilute hydrocarbon concentrations below LEL.

5. TROUBLESHOOTING MATRIX:
- Vibration excursion on DE (> 3.8 mm/s): Liquid carryover into 1st stage impeller. Check KO drum LT-104.
- Bearing temp DE rising (> 90.0 °C): Lube oil cooler fouled or low flow. Switch filter basket, increase cooling water.
- Seal gas DP dropping (< 3.2 bar delta): Primary vent restriction or booster failure. Verify N2 backup manifold.
- MOV-101 fails to reach closed state: Torque limit trip or mechanical obstruction. Engage manual handwheel declutch.
