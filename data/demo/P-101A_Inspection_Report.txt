2.0 RECENT PROCESS DISTURBANCE & EMERGENCY INTERVENTION SUMMARY
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
At 18:05 IST during routine gasoil charging, a sudden downstream control valve stiction
event induced rapid hydraulic pressure accumulation in discharge spool 12-CDU-401-HC.
- Discharge Pressure Surge   : Peaked at 492.5 PSI (exceeded 450.0 PSI trip set-point).
- Safety Action 1            : Interlock I-101 tripped 6.6 kV breaker VCB-101A within 42 ms.
- Safety Action 2            : Emergency Relief Valve SV-402 actuated open to Flare Header.
- Inboard Bearing Temp (TT)  : Spiked to 96.2 deg C (Trip threshold: 95.0 deg C).
- Post-Trip Hydraulic Status : System safely depressurized to 94.0 PSI within 35 minutes.
- Containment Verification   : Zero hydrocarbon leakage detected at casing joints or seal pot.
